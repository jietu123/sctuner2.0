Golden Rules v2.1 update

Golden Rule 18:
No reliable formal baseline mapping, no SVTuner biological application.

A candidate cannot enter BioApp Phase 2 unless formal baseline mapping feasibility is supported.
The candidate must support:
1. identifiable ST mapping object
2. usable scRNA-seq reference
3. sufficient gene intersection
4. compatible preprocessing
5. baseline CytoSPACE feasibility
6. SVTuner-enhanced mapping feasibility
7. identical input basis for baseline and SVTuner
8. endpoint-specific comparison after mapping

If external endpoint is feasible but mapping mainline is not feasible, the candidate must be downgraded to:
endpoint feasibility only, not main biological application.
